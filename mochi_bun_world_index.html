Top recipe — Mochi Lemon Bites

  1. Mix sweet rice flour and warm water to form a soft dough.
  2. Shape into little buns, steam 6–8 minutes.
  3. Top with lemon cream or a pastel glaze. Enjoy!
Easy to make and perfect for sharing photos.
